We then saw Nishat Bagh and Mughal Gardens. We spent the evening on a luxury 4-star houseboat in Nigeen Lake and took a Shikara ride.
  > On the 7th day, we flew back to Pune via Mumbai, carrying beautiful memories. Bandhan Tours made honest efforts, checking in periodically. One must accept unexpected issues during travel, but improvements are needed to ensure promised services (like the Gondola). Tour Manager Subodh Bhise managed the tour very well.
