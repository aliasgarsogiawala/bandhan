Special Kerala

A compact seven-day introduction to Kerala's hills, backwaters and coast.

Overview

The Journey at a Glance

Munnar's tea gardens and Thekkady's traditional performances, Varkala's cliffside beach and Kovalam's temples, ending with a houseboat cruise through Alleppey's backwaters.

Munnar tea gardens & Eravikulam National Park
Kathakali & Kalaripayattu shows
Varkala cliffside beach
Alleppey houseboat backwaters
Day by Day

Detailed Itinerary

01

Cheeyappara Waterfalls en route, then the Tea Museum.

Meals: Lunch, DinnerOvernight: Munnar
02

Eravikulam National Park, tea gardens, Mattupetty Dam and Kundala Lake.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, DinnerOvernight: Munnar
03

Spice plantations, then an evening Kathakali Dance and Kalaripayattu show.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, DinnerOvernight: Thekkady
04

Jatayu Earth's Center, then Varkala's cliffside beach.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, DinnerOvernight: Varkala
05

Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple, Kuthiramalika and Napier Museums, local markets.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, DinnerOvernight: Kovalam
06

Board a traditional houseboat for a backwater cruise.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, DinnerOvernight: Alleppey houseboat
07

Check out from the houseboat and transfer to Cochin.

Meals: BreakfastOvernight: —
