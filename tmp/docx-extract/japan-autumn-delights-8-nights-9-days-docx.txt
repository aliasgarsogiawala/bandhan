Japan Autumn Delights – 9 Nights / 10 Days
Departure dates: 16 Nov 2026
Routing – Tokyo - Mt. Fuji - Mishima - Nagoya - Nara - Kyoto - Kobe - Hiroshima - Okayama - Osaka
Itinerary:
Day 1 | 16th Nov - ARRIVAL IN TOKYO
Welcome to Japan! Upon arrival at Narita International Airport, complete the immigration and baggage formalities before meeting your representative for a private transfer to your hotel in Tokyo.
After check-in, take some time to relax and recover from your journey. In the evening, enjoy a delicious Indian dinner at a local restaurant.
Meals: Dinner
Note: Sightseeing is subject to your flight arrival time.
Day 2 | 17th Nov - TOKYO – FULL DAY TOUR
After breakfast, depart from the hotel at 09:30 Hrs for a full-day exploration of Tokyo, a fascinating city where centuries-old traditions blend seamlessly with futuristic innovation.
Tokyo Skytree (Admission Included)
Visit the 350-metre observation deck and admire spectacular panoramic views of Tokyo's skyline.
Sensō-ji
Explore Tokyo's oldest and most revered Buddhist temple.
Nakamise Shopping Street
Walk through this lively traditional shopping street filled with local delicacies, souvenirs, and Japanese handicrafts.
Shibuya Scramble Crossing
Drive past the world's most famous pedestrian crossing and experience the vibrant atmosphere of Shibuya.
TeamLab Planets TOKYO DMM (Admission Included)
Experience an extraordinary world of immersive digital art through interactive installations.
After completing the sightseeing, return to the hotel.
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner
Day 3 | 18th Nov - TOKYO – MOUNT FUJI – FULL DAY TOUR
After breakfast, check out from the hotel and depart at 09:00 Hrs for a scenic excursion to the iconic Mount Fuji region, known for its stunning natural beauty and unforgettable experiences.
Mount Fuji 5th Station (Weather Permitting)
Visit the famous 5th Station of Japan's highest mountain and enjoy breathtaking views of the surrounding landscapes.
Sumo Show & Experience (Admission Included)
Discover Japan's national sport through an engaging and interactive sumo wrestling experience.
Mt. Fuji Panoramic Ropeway (Admission Included)
Enjoy a scenic ropeway ride offering magnificent views of Mount Fuji and Lake Kawaguchi.
Later, continue to Mishima and check in to your hotel.
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner
Day 4 | 19th Nov - MISHIMA – FULL DAY TOUR – NAGOYA
After breakfast, check out from the hotel and depart at 09:00 Hrs for Nagoya, a city renowned for its industrial innovation and cultural heritage.
Toyota Commemorative Museum of Industry and Technology (Admission Included)
Learn about Toyota's remarkable journey from textile machinery to becoming one of the world's leading automobile manufacturers through interactive exhibits.
Nabana no Sato (Admission Included)
Visit one of Japan's most beautiful botanical gardens, famous for its colourful seasonal flowers, picturesque gardens, and spectacular illumination displays.
Continue to Nagoya and check in to your hotel.
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner
Day 5 | 20th Nov - NAGOYA – NARA – OSAKA
After breakfast, begin your journey towards Nara before continuing to Osaka, discovering some of Japan's most iconic cultural landmarks along the way.
Kimono Wearing Experience (Admission Included)
Dress in a traditional Japanese kimono and capture memorable photographs while experiencing an important part of Japanese culture.
Todaiji Temple (Admission Included)
Visit one of Japan's most celebrated temples, home to the magnificent Great Buddha statue.
Nara Deer Park (Visit)
Enjoy a stroll through the famous park where hundreds of friendly deer roam freely.
Umeda Sky Building – Floating Garden Observatory (Admission Included)
End the day with panoramic views of Osaka's impressive skyline from one of the city's most iconic observation decks.
Proceed to your hotel in Osaka.
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner
Day 6 | 21st Nov - OSAKA – KYOTO – FULL DAY TOUR – OSAKA
After breakfast, depart at 09:00 Hrs for a full-day excursion to Kyoto, Japan's ancient capital, celebrated for its timeless temples, rich heritage, and beautiful landscapes.
Arashiyama Bamboo Grove (Visit)
Walk through the famous bamboo forest and experience its peaceful natural surroundings.
Sagano Romantic Train (Admission Included)
Enjoy a scenic train journey through the picturesque Hozugawa River valley with beautiful seasonal views.
Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion) (Admission Included)
Visit Kyoto's magnificent Golden Pavilion, surrounded by beautifully landscaped gardens and a tranquil pond.
Fushimi Inari Taisha (Visit)
Explore one of Japan's most iconic shrines, famous for its thousands of vibrant vermilion torii gates.
Return to Osaka after the sightseeing.
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner
Day 7 | 22nd Nov - OSAKA – KOBE – FULL DAY TOUR – OKAYAMA
After breakfast, check out from the hotel and depart at 09:00 Hrs for Kobe, a charming port city known for its scenic landscapes and cosmopolitan atmosphere. Later, continue to Okayama.
Mount Rokko Cable Car (One-Way Admission Included)
Enjoy a scenic cable car ride with panoramic views of Kobe, Osaka Bay, and the surrounding mountains.
Himeji Castle (Admission Included)
Visit Japan's finest feudal castle and UNESCO World Heritage Site, admired for its elegant white architecture and beautifully preserved grounds.
Continue to Okayama and check in to your hotel.
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner
Day 8 | 23rd Nov - OSAKA – HIROSHIMA – FULL DAY TOUR – OKAYAMA
After breakfast, depart at 09:00 Hrs for Hiroshima, a city that stands as a symbol of resilience, peace, and hope. Later, board the famous Shinkansen to Okayama.
Hiroshima Peace Memorial Museum (Admission Included)
Explore powerful exhibits that preserve the memory of Hiroshima while promoting peace.
Atomic Bomb Dome, Cenotaph & Sadako Monument (Visit)
Visit Hiroshima Peace Memorial Park and its significant monuments dedicated to the victims and the message of world peace.
Gandhi Statue (Visit)
See the statue of Mahatma Gandhi, representing peace, harmony, and non-violence.
Miyajima Ferry Return (Admission Included)
Itsukushima Shrine & Floating Torii Gate (Admission Included / Visit)
Visit the UNESCO World Heritage-listed shrine, renowned for its iconic floating torii gate.
Shinkansen (Bullet Train) to Okayama (Admission Included)
Experience Japan's world-famous high-speed rail network during your comfortable journey to Okayama.
Upon arrival, transfer to your hotel.
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner
Day 9 | 24th Nov - OKAYAMA – OSAKA – FULL DAY TOUR – KANSAI AIRPORT
After breakfast, check out and depart at 09:00 Hrs for Osaka, where you'll enjoy a day of sightseeing, shopping, and entertainment before proceeding to the Kansai Airport area.
Dotonbori & Shinsaibashi-suji Shopping Street (Shopping)
Explore Osaka's lively shopping district, famous for its vibrant atmosphere, fashion boutiques, local delicacies, and dazzling neon lights.
Osaka Kaiyukan Aquarium (Admission Included)
Visit one of the world's largest aquariums, home to an incredible variety of marine life, including the famous whale shark.
Rinku Premium Outlets (Shopping)
Enjoy some last-minute shopping at one of Japan's largest outlet malls featuring renowned international and Japanese brands.
Proceed to your hotel near Kansai Airport.
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner
Day 10 | 25th Nov – DEPART
After breakfast, check out from the hotel and prepare for your onward journey.
Guests may use the complimentary hotel shuttle service to Kansai International Airport according to their respective flight schedules. The hotel is conveniently located approximately 20 minutes from the airport, ensuring a smooth and comfortable transfer.
Guests with later departures may spend some leisure time at the nearby Kansai Outlet Mall, located within walking distance of the hotel, making it an ideal place for some last-minute shopping before heading to the airport.
Meals: Breakfast
