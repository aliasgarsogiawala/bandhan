Itinerary:
Day 1 | Arrival in Paris – The City of Romance, Lights & Glamour
Welcome to Paris, one of Europe’s most elegant and enchanting cities, renowned for its haute couture, world-famous museums, magnificent monuments and vibrant culture. Upon arrival, complete your immigration formalities and collect your baggage. Proceed outside the airport, where you will meet your professional Tour Manager. Later, transfer to your hotel and check in. Relax and enjoy the comforts of your hotel after your journey.
Overnight stay in Paris.
Meals: Dinner
Day 2 | Paris City Tour – Eiffel Tower – Versailles – Seine Cruise – Paris by Night
After breakfast, proceed for a guided city tour of Paris. Drive past and admire some of the city's most iconic landmarks, including Place Vendôme, Opéra Garnier, Musée d’Orsay, Place de la Concorde, Champs-Élysées, Arc de Triomphe, Alexander Bridge and Les Invalides. Next, visit the world-famous Eiffel Tower and ascend to the 3rd Level (Top Level) for spectacular panoramic views over Paris. The Eiffel Tower, one of the most recognizable landmarks in the world, offers a memorable view of the city's beautiful skyline. Continue to the magnificent Palace of Versailles, a masterpiece of French architecture and a symbol of the grandeur of the French monarchy. Explore its magnificent surroundings and admire its rich artistic and historical heritage. Later, enjoy a relaxing cruise on the River Seine, passing some of Paris's most celebrated monuments, including the Eiffel Tower, Notre-Dame and Musée d’Orsay. In the evening, experience Paris by Night, when the city's famous landmarks are beautifully illuminated. Drive past attractions such as the Eiffel Tower, Champs-Élysées, Arc de Triomphe and Place de la Concorde, witnessing the magical atmosphere of the City of Light.
Important Note: Access to the Eiffel Tower's 3rd Level is subject to operational availability. In case the 3rd Level is closed, the visit will be arranged up to the 2nd Level, offering equally impressive views of Paris. Overnight stay in Paris.
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner
Day 3 | Disneyland® Paris – A Day of Fun & Adventure
Today, get ready for an exciting day at Disneyland® Paris. Choose between the Disney® Park or Walt Disney Studios® Park. At Disney® Park, enter a magical world filled with fairy-tale attractions, thrilling rides, entertaining shows and Disney Characters. Alternatively, explore Walt Disney Studios® Park and discover the world of movies, exciting attractions, stunt shows and behind-the-scenes experiences. Enjoy a fun-filled day with your family and loved ones before returning to the hotel. Overnight stay in Paris.
Meals: Breakfast, Packed Lunch & Dinner
Day 4 | Paris – Geneva Orientation Tour
After breakfast, check out and proceed towards Switzerland, a beautiful country known for its spectacular Alpine scenery, pristine lakes and charming cities. On arrival in Geneva, enjoy an orientation tour of this elegant Swiss city. See the famous Jet d’Eau, one of Geneva's most recognizable landmarks, the United Nations Office, and the beautiful Flower Clock located near Lake Geneva. Later, proceed to your hotel and check in. Relax and enjoy the peaceful surroundings of Switzerland. Overnight stay in Geneva.
Meals: Breakfast, Packed Lunch & Dinner
Day 5 | Jungfraujoch – Top of Europe – Grindelwald – Interlaken
Today, get ready for one of the highlights of your European journey – an unforgettable excursion to Jungfraujoch – The Top of Europe. Proceed towards Grindelwald Terminal and board the spectacular Eiger Express 3S cable car, which takes you to Eigergletscher in approximately 15 minutes. From there, continue by cogwheel train to Jungfraujoch, home to Europe's highest railway station at approximately 3,454 metres above sea level. Explore the fascinating Ice Palace, where impressive sculptures are created from ice. Continue to the Sphinx Observatory, offering breathtaking panoramic views of the surrounding snow-covered Alps and the Aletsch Glacier. Later, enjoy the scenic beauty of the Swiss Alpine region, including the picturesque surroundings of Interlaken, before proceeding to your hotel. Overnight stay in Central Switzerland.
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner
Day 6 | Mount Titlis – Lucerne – Lake Lucerne Cruise
After breakfast, proceed for an exciting excursion to Mount Titlis, located at approximately 3,020 metres above sea level. Enjoy a series of scenic cable car rides, including the famous Titlis Rotair, the world's first revolving cable car. From the summit, admire breathtaking 360-degree views of the snow-covered Swiss Alps, glaciers and dramatic mountain landscapes. Experience the spectacular Cliff Walk, one of Europe's highest suspension bridges, built along the cliffs of Mount Titlis. Later, proceed to Lucerne, one of Switzerland's most picturesque cities. Enjoy an orientation tour covering the famous Lion Monument and Chapel Bridge (Kapellbrücke). You will also have some free time to shop for famous Swiss watches, chocolates and souvenirs. Later, enjoy a relaxing and scenic cruise on Lake Lucerne, surrounded by magnificent Alpine scenery. Overnight stay in Central Switzerland.
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner
Day 7 | Rhine Falls – Vaduz – Swarovski Crystal World – Innsbruck
After breakfast, check out and proceed towards Schaffhausen to visit the magnificent Rhine Falls, one of Europe's most spectacular waterfalls. Enjoy a thrilling boat ride at Rhine Falls, bringing you closer to the powerful cascading waters and offering an unforgettable experience.
Continue towards Vaduz, the capital of the tiny Principality of Liechtenstein, beautifully located between Switzerland and Austria. Enjoy a guided mini-train ride through Vaduz and admire its charming surroundings. Later, proceed to Wattens and visit the fascinating Swarovski Crystal Worlds, where you can discover an impressive collection of crystal artworks and creative installations. Continue your journey to Innsbruck, the picturesque capital of Tyrol in Austria. During the orientation tour, see the famous Golden Roof, one of Innsbruck's most recognizable landmarks, and stroll along Maria-Theresien-Strasse, surrounded by historic architecture and the magnificent Alps. Overnight stay in Innsbruck / Seefeld.
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner
Day 8 | Innsbruck – Venice – Gondola Ride
After breakfast, check out and proceed towards Venice, the enchanting floating city of Italy, famous for its canals, bridges, historic architecture and romantic atmosphere.
On arrival, board a private boat to reach St. Mark's Square, the heart of Venice. During the walking tour, view St. Mark's Basilica, St. Mark's Bell Tower, the Clock Tower and the famous Bridge of Sighs. Later, enjoy one of Venice's most memorable experiences – a traditional Gondola Ride through the picturesque Venetian canals. Glide past historic buildings and charming waterways while experiencing the unique atmosphere of this beautiful city. Later, return to the pier and proceed to your hotel for check-in. Overnight stay in Padova / Ferrara.
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner
Day 9 | Florence Walking Tour – Pisa – Leaning Tower
After breakfast, check out and proceed towards Florence, the birthplace of the Renaissance. Enjoy a guided walking tour of this historic city. See the magnificent Duomo, Campanile and Baptistery, along with Piazza della Signoria, an open-air museum featuring impressive Renaissance sculptures. Continue to the famous Ponte Vecchio, the historic bridge spanning the River Arno and one of Florence's most iconic landmarks. Later, proceed towards Pisa and visit the famous Square of Miracles (Piazza dei Miracoli). Admire the world-renowned Leaning Tower of Pisa, one of Italy's most recognizable monuments. Continue towards your hotel in the Tuscany region. Overnight stay in Tuscany Region.
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner
Day 10 | Florence / Tuscany – Rome – Vatican City – Colosseum – Trevi Fountain
After breakfast, proceed towards Rome, the Eternal City and one of the world's most historically significant destinations.
On arrival, visit Vatican City, the world's smallest independent country. Explore the magnificent St. Peter's Basilica, renowned for its extraordinary architecture and masterpieces by celebrated artists including Michelangelo and Bernini.
Continue your sightseeing in Rome with a visit to the iconic Colosseum, the ancient amphitheatre and one of the most famous symbols of the Roman Empire.
Later, visit the beautiful Trevi Fountain. According to tradition, tossing a coin into the fountain is believed to ensure your return to Rome. Proceed to your hotel and check in. Overnight stay in Rome.
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner
Day 11 | Rome – Departure – Fly Back Home
After breakfast, check out from the hotel and proceed to the airport for your return flight.
As your memorable European holiday comes to an end, say goodbye to your Tour Manager and fellow travellers and take home wonderful memories of Paris, Switzerland, Liechtenstein, Austria and Italy that you will cherish for a lifetime.
Meals: Breakfast
