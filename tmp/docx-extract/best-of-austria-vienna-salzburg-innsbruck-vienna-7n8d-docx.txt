Itinerary:
Day 1: Arrival in Vienna
Welcome to Austria! Upon arrival at Vienna International Airport, you will be met by your private transfer and driven to your hotel. Check-in is (from 1500 hrs). Spend the rest of the day at leisure exploring the nearby streets, relaxing at a café, or simply enjoying the elegant atmosphere of Austria's capital on own. Overnight stay in Vienna.
Day 2: Explore Vienna
After breakfast, make your way to the designated meeting point to begin your 24-Hour Hop-On Hop-Off City Tour. Discover Vienna's iconic landmarks at your own pace, including the State Opera, Parliament, City Hall, and St. Stephen's Cathedral. Visit the magnificent Schönbrunn Palace with your included entrance ticket (without guide) and explore its grand gardens and imperial interiors. Overnight stay in Vienna.
Day 3: Vienna – Salzburg
Enjoy breakfast at the hotel before checking out. A private transfer will take you to Vienna Train Station for your comfortable (2nd Class) train journey to Salzburg. Upon arrival, another private transfer will escort you to your hotel. Check-in is (from 1500 hrs). Spend the evening strolling through Salzburg's charming Old Town, famous for its Baroque architecture and musical heritage. Overnight stay in Salzburg.
Day 4: Hallstatt & Salt Mine Experience
After breakfast, proceed to the designated meeting point for your shared shuttle transfer to the famous Salt Mine. Explore one of the world’s oldest salt mines and enjoy the fascinating underground experience. Later, you can spend time exploring the picturesque lakeside surroundings on your own before returning to Salzburg. Overnight stay in Salzburg.
Day 5: Salzburg – Innsbruck
After breakfast, check out from the hotel. A private transfer will take you to Salzburg Train Station for your train journey to Innsbruck (2nd Class). Upon arrival, meet your private transfer and proceed to your hotel. Check-in is (from 1500 hrs). The remainder of the day is free to explore Innsbruck's charming Old Town or enjoy shopping at the local boutiques. Overnight stay in Innsbruck.
Day 6: Top of Innsbruck & Swarovski Crystal Worlds
Enjoy breakfast before heading to the meeting point for your shared transfer to the world-famous Swarovski Crystal Worlds. Discover the dazzling crystal exhibits and beautifully landscaped gardens. Later, experience the spectacular Top of Innsbruck with your round-trip cable car ticket, offering breathtaking panoramic views of the surrounding Alps. Overnight stay in Innsbruck.
Day 7: Innsbruck – Vienna
After breakfast, check out of your hotel and take a private transfer to Innsbruck Train Station. Board your train to Vienna (2nd Class). Upon arrival, a private transfer will take you to your hotel. Check-in is (from 1500 hrs) and enjoy the remainder of the day shopping or exploring Vienna’s elegant shopping streets and cafés at your own pace. Overnight stay in Vienna.
Day 8: Departure from Vienna
After breakfast, check out from the hotel. A private transfer will take you to Vienna International Airport for your onward flight home, taking back wonderful memories of your Austrian holiday. Safe travels!
