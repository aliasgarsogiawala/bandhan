Itinerary:
Day 1: Arrival in Oslo
Welcome to Norway! Upon arrival at Oslo Airport, meet your tour manager and transfer to your hotel. Norway's vibrant capital blends Scandinavian charm with modern architecture, Viking heritage, and stunning waterfront views. The rest of the day is at leisure to relax after your journey or explore the nearby surroundings. In the evening, enjoy dinner followed by a short tour briefing. Overnight stay in Oslo.
Day 2: Oslo Fjord Cruise & Holmenkollen Ski Jump
After breakfast, begin the day with a scenic cruise on the beautiful Oslo Fjord, sailing past charming islands, picturesque harbors, and the city’s impressive skyline. Later, visit the iconic Holmenkollen Ski Jump, one of Norway’s most famous landmarks, offering spectacular panoramic views over Oslo and the surrounding fjord. Explore the historic Ski Museum, showcasing over 4,000 years of skiing history. The afternoon is free to explore the city or visit nearby attractions at your own pace before returning to the hotel. Overnight stay in Oslo.
Day 3: Oslo City Tour – Stockholm
Enjoy breakfast before setting out on an orientation tour of Oslo, passing the Royal Palace, Karl Johans Gate, Oslo City Hall, Parliament House, and the striking Oslo Opera House. Later, depart by luxury coach for Stockholm, enjoying scenic Nordic landscapes filled with forests, lakes, and charming villages along the way. Upon arrival in Sweden's elegant capital, check in to your hotel and relax before dinner. Overnight stay in Stockholm.
Day 4: Stockholm City Tour & Overnight Baltic Cruise
After breakfast, discover the highlights of Stockholm on a guided city tour. Explore the charming cobbled streets of Gamla Stan (Old Town), admire the Royal Palace, Stockholm Cathedral, City Hall, and enjoy beautiful views from Fjällgatan. After some free time, transfer to the port and board your overnight Baltic Sea cruise to Helsinki. Enjoy dinner on board while sailing through the stunning archipelago. Overnight onboard the cruise.
Day 5: Helsinki City Tour & Porvoo
Arrive in Helsinki after breakfast and begin a guided city tour covering Senate Square, Helsinki Cathedral, the remarkable Rock Church (Temppeliaukio Church), the Sibelius Monument, and Uspenski Cathedral. Continue to the charming medieval town of Porvoo, famous for its colorful wooden houses, cobbled streets, riverside views, and historic cathedral. Return to Helsinki in the evening and relax at your hotel. Overnight stay in Helsinki.
Day 6: Tallinn Excursion & Santa Claus Express
After breakfast, board a high-speed ferry to Tallinn, Estonia’s enchanting medieval capital. Explore the UNESCO-listed Old Town, including Toompea Castle, Alexander Nevsky Cathedral, Town Hall Square, and its charming narrow streets lined with cafés and boutiques. Later, return to Helsinki by ferry. In the evening, board the legendary Santa Claus Express overnight train to Rovaniemi, travelling across the Arctic Circle while you sleep. Overnight onboard the Santa Claus Express.
Day 7: Ranua Wildlife Park & Northern Lights Experience
Arrive in Rovaniemi and begin your Arctic adventure with a visit to Ranua Wildlife Park, Finland’s northernmost zoo. Walk through snowy forest trails to observe Arctic wildlife including polar bears, Arctic foxes, wolves, lynx, moose, and snowy owls in their natural surroundings. Later, return to Rovaniemi and, after dinner, head out on an exciting Northern Lights excursion. If weather conditions are favorable, witness the magical Aurora Borealis illuminating the Arctic sky in shades of green, pink, and violet. Overnight stay in Rovaniemi.
Day 8: Santa Claus Village & Husky Safari
After breakfast, visit the world-famous Santa Claus Village, where Christmas is celebrated every day of the year. Cross the Arctic Circle, meet Santa Claus, visit his official post office, and browse festive souvenir shops. Later, visit a traditional Husky Farm, meet the energetic Siberian huskies, and enjoy an exhilarating husky sled ride through the snow-covered forests (subject to snow conditions). Return to the hotel for a farewell dinner. Overnight stay in Rovaniemi.
Day 9: Departure from Rovaniemi
After breakfast, check out from the hotel and transfer to Rovaniemi Airport for your onward flight home. Depart with unforgettable memories of Scandinavia's vibrant cities, breathtaking fjords, medieval towns, Arctic adventures, magical Northern Lights, and the enchanting home of Santa Claus. Safe travels!
