Itinerary:
Day 1: Arrival in Baku & Panoramic City Tour
Welcome to Azerbaijan! Upon arrival at Heydar Aliyev International Airport, meet your local representative and transfer to your hotel. After check-in and some time to relax, assemble in the evening for a panoramic city tour. Visit Highland Park (Alley of Martyrs), offering breathtaking views of Baku's skyline and the Caspian Sea. Continue past the National Assembly (Milli Majlis) and admire the magnificent Flame Towers, the city's most iconic modern landmarks. Return to the hotel for an overnight stay in Baku.
Day 2: Discover Historic & Modern Baku
After breakfast, begin your exploration of Icherisheher (Old City), the UNESCO World Heritage-listed historic heart of Baku. Wander through its ancient stone streets while visiting landmarks such as the Maiden Tower and the Palace of the Shirvanshahs, showcasing Azerbaijan's rich cultural heritage. Later, enjoy a photo stop at the stunning Heydar Aliyev Center, an architectural masterpiece designed by renowned architect Zaha Hadid. Continue with a relaxing walk along Baku Boulevard, stretching along the beautiful Caspian Sea waterfront. Overnight stay in Baku.
Day 3: Gabala Tour
After breakfast, depart for the scenic mountain town of Gabala, located in northwestern Azerbaijan. Surrounded by the majestic Greater Caucasus Mountains, Gabala is one of the country's most beautiful destinations. Visit the Tufandag Mountain Resort, where you can enjoy a cable car ride offering spectacular mountain views (activities at own expense unless included). Later, visit the tranquil Nohur Lake, a picturesque alpine lake surrounded by forests and mountains, perfect for photography and relaxation.  evening. Overnight stay in Gabala.
Day 4: Qobustan & Deniz Mall
Enjoy breakfast before departing for Gobustan National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage Site famous for its ancient rock carvings dating back thousands of years. Explore fascinating prehistoric petroglyphs depicting hunting scenes, wildlife, and early human life. Later, visit the modern Deniz Mall, one of Baku's premier shopping and entertainment destinations overlooking the Caspian Sea. Spend time shopping, dining, or relaxing before returning to your hotel. Overnight stay in Baku.
Day 5: Flames Tour
After breakfast, visit the historic Ateshgah Fire Temple, once an important place of worship for Zoroastrians and Hindu pilgrims, known for its eternal flames fueled by natural gas. Continue to Yanardag (Burning Mountain), where natural gas flames have burned continuously for centuries, giving Azerbaijan its famous nickname, the "Land of Fire." Later, enjoy free time at Ganjlik Mall, one of Baku's largest shopping and entertainment centers. Overnight stay in Baku.
Day 6: Shahdag Tour
After breakfast, travel to the spectacular Shahdag Mountain Resort, nestled in the Greater Caucasus Mountains. As Azerbaijan's first and largest mountain resort, Shahdag offers breathtaking alpine scenery and a wide range of seasonal activities such as cable car rides, skiing, snow activities (winter), ziplining, mountain coaster, ATV rides, and other adventure experiences (activities subject to season and availability). After enjoying the mountain resort, return to Baku in the evening. Overnight stay in Baku.
Day 7: Departure from Baku
After breakfast, check out from the hotel and transfer to Heydar Aliyev International Airport for your onward flight. Depart Azerbaijan with unforgettable memories of its ancient heritage, modern architecture, scenic mountain landscapes, and unique natural wonders. Safe travels!
