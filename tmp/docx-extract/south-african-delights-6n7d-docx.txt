Itinerary:
Day 1: Arrival in Cape Town
Welcome to South Africa! Upon arrival at Cape Town International Airport, you will be greeted by your local representative and transferred to your hotel. Set between the majestic Table Mountain and the sparkling Atlantic Ocean, Cape Town is one of the world's most beautiful cities, known for its stunning landscapes, rich history, and vibrant culture. After check-in, the remainder of the day is free to relax or explore the city's lively waterfront, cafés, and nearby attractions at your own pace. Overnight stay in Cape Town.
Day 2: Cape Town City Tour & Table Mountain
After breakfast, set out on a guided Half-Day Mother City Tour. Drive through the scenic suburbs of Clifton and Sea Point before visiting some of Cape Town’s most iconic landmarks, including the Houses of Parliament, Castle of Good Hope, South African Museum, District Six, Slave Lodge, the colorful Bo-Kaap neighborhood, and Greenmarket Square. Later, ascend the world-famous Table Mountain by cable car (weather permitting) and enjoy breathtaking panoramic views of Cape Town, Table Bay, and the surrounding coastline. If weather conditions prevent cable car operations, a visit to Signal Hill will be offered as an alternative. Overnight stay in Cape Town.
Day 3: Cape Peninsula & Cape of Good Hope
After breakfast, embark on a full-day excursion along the spectacular Cape Peninsula. Travel via the scenic Atlantic coastline, passing through Sea Point, Camps Bay, Clifton, Llandudno, Hout Bay, Noordhoek, and Scarborough before reaching the famous Cape of Good Hope Nature Reserve. Enjoy a ride on the Flying Dutchman Funicular and admire the dramatic coastal scenery. Continue to Boulders Beach, home to a delightful colony of African Penguins, and visit the historic naval town of Simon’s Town, where you’ll learn about the legendary Able Seaman Just Nuisance. Overnight stay in Cape Town.
Day 4: Cape Town – Johannesburg – Sun City
After breakfast, transfer to Cape Town International Airport for your flight to Johannesburg (flight not included). Upon arrival, meet your representative and continue by road to the renowned Sun City Resort, South Africa's premier leisure destination. Surrounded by lush gardens, Swimming pool and world-class recreational facilities, Sun City offers a variety of attractions including the Valley of Waves, the Lost City, golf courses, and entertainment complexes. Spend the remainder of the day at leisure enjoying the resort's many amenities. Overnight stay in Sun City.
Day 5: Leisure in Sun City
Enjoy breakfast at the hotel before spending the day at leisure. Relax by the pool, explore the resort, or choose an optional safari in the nearby Pilanesberg National Park, one of South Africa’s leading wildlife reserves and home to the famous Big Five (optional and at additional cost). Overnight stay in Sun City.
Day 6: Sun City – Johannesburg & Gold Reef City
After breakfast, depart for Johannesburg. Upon arrival, enjoy a guided visit to Gold Reef City, a unique theme park and museum built around Johannesburg's rich gold mining history. Discover reconstructed mining villages, historical exhibits, fascinating gold-pouring demonstrations, entertainment attractions, and cultural experiences that showcase the city's golden heritage. Later, transfer to your hotel in Johannesburg. Overnight stay in Johannesburg.
Day 7: Departure from Johannesburg
After breakfast, check out from the hotel and transfer to O.R. Tambo International Airport for your onward flight. Depart South Africa with unforgettable memories of its breathtaking landscapes, incredible wildlife, vibrant cities, and unforgettable experiences. Safe travels!
