Itinerary:
Day 1 | Arrival in Istanbul
Upon arrival at Istanbul International Airport, meet your local representative and transfer to your hotel. After check-in, relax and enjoy the rest of the day at leisure. Istanbul, the only city spanning both Europe and Asia, is renowned for its rich history, magnificent monuments, vibrant bazaars, and unique cultural heritage. Overnight stay in Istanbul.
Day 2 | Istanbul City Tour
After breakfast, proceed for a guided city tour of Istanbul. Visit the historic Hippodrome, followed by the magnificent Blue Mosque, famous for its beautiful blue Iznik tiles. Continue to the iconic Hagia Sophia Grand Mosque, one of the finest examples of Byzantine architecture. Visit a traditional shopping centre where you can explore Turkish delights, spices, ceramics, handicrafts, souvenirs, and local products. Return to the hotel for an overnight stay.
Note: Please carry a headscarf for visits to the Blue Mosque and Hagia Sophia.
Day 3 | Istanbul – Denizli – Pamukkale
After breakfast, enjoy free time until your transfer to Istanbul Airport for your domestic flight to Denizli. Upon arrival, transfer to Pamukkale and check in to your hotel. Spend the evening at leisure. Overnight stay in Pamukkale.
Day 4 | Pamukkale Tour – Antalya
After breakfast, visit the spectacular white travertine terraces of Pamukkale and explore the ancient ruins of Hierapolis, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Enjoy time to admire the natural thermal pools before visiting a local textile factory. Later, proceed to Antalya for hotel check-in. Overnight stay in Antalya.
Optional: Experience an early morning Hot Air Balloon ride over Pamukkale (at an additional cost).
Day 5 | Antalya Old City Tour)
After breakfast, explore the historic Kaleiçi (Old Town) of Antalya. Visit landmarks including Hadrian's Gate, Clock Tower, Broken Minaret, Hıdırlık Tower, and the charming Marina area. Later, visit the beautiful Düden Waterfalls, where the waters flow into the Mediterranean Sea. Return to the hotel and overnight stay.
Day 6 | Antalya – Cappadocia
After breakfast, transfer to Antalya Airport for your domestic flight to Cappadocia. Upon arrival, transfer to your hotel and check in. The remainder of the day is free to relax or explore the unique surroundings at your own pace. Overnight stay in Cappadocia.
Optional: Register for the famous Cappadocia Hot Air Balloon ride scheduled for the following morning (at an additional cost).
Day 7 | Cappadocia Tour
After breakfast, enjoy a full-day tour of Cappadocia. Visit the fascinating Ozkonak Underground City, the UNESCO-listed Göreme Open Air Museum, the cave village of Çavuşin, and a traditional pottery workshop in Avanos. Continue to the scenic Love Valley, Devrent Valley (Imagination Valley), and the famous Three Beauties Fairy Chimneys, the symbol of Cappadocia. Return to the hotel for Overnight stay.
Optional: Sunrise Hot Air Balloon ride over Cappadocia (at an additional cost).
Day 8 | Cappadocia – Istanbul – Departure
After breakfast, enjoy some free time for shopping before transferring to Kayseri or Nevşehir Airport for your domestic flight to Istanbul. Connect with your onward international flight back home, taking with you wonderful memories of Turkey. Have a safe journey!
